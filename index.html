Users and roles

Officer: sees and works on one LGA. State: sees both LGAs (HMIS focal person). Admin: everything, including this page. Facility: records officer who uses Snap Check (snapcheck-osun.netlify.app) for one facility (set LGA and facility). Doctor: checks death certificates in the MCCD Checker (mccd-app.netlify.app) (set the hospital, e.g. Wesley Guild Hospital). Coder: HIM staff who review and code certificates for one hospital (set the hospital). Pending: no access.
